Voici les deux paragraphes en anglais pour la nouvelle version, puis le fichier complet. **Costa Agent** is a mobile web app, saved to an iPhone home screen, that works as a knowledge and call companion for a Smartmove property consultant covering the Costa del Sol from Málaga to Sotogrande. It profiles 17 zones. Each profile gives the average asking price per m² and its yearly change, the time to Málaga airport and the typical buyers. It also lists neighbourhood "pockets," key streets, urbanizations, golf courses, schools, beaches and landmarks, with pros and watch-outs. Each zone comes with a ready-made pitch that adapts to the buyer profile: family, golfer, investor, retiree, remote worker or luxury. A colour-coded map shows the zones by price tier. The Guide adds a price ranking, a glossary of Spanish property terms and short answers to common client objections, such as the Golden Visa, the proposed non-EU tax or "Marbella is too expensive." The app also helps the consultant learn the market and answer clients quickly, without holding any client data. She builds a list of new developments, one a day: name, zone, developer, starting price, delivery date and a one-line pitch. One of them appears each morning as the "development of the day," and each one turns into flashcards, alongside decks on Spanish terms, zone prices and streets, with an export to Quizlet. An anonymous budget tool shows what a budget buys in each zone and which zones fit or are a stretch. It also lists the developments in her list that start under that budget, and copies a short summary to paste into Monday. Other tools estimate buying costs using Smartmove's ranges, check a listing's price against its zone, give rough mortgage and rental-yield figures, and convert pounds or dollars to euros and m² to square feet. Monday remains the only place for client records.
